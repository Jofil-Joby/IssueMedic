## Decision and Reasoning

IssueMedic makes an assessment by analyzing evidence related to issue-management configuration. It connects detected problems to supporting evidence and practical actions.

## Inputs and Data Sources

IssueMedic uses source files, configuration, project structure, and relevant issue-management configuration data from the inspected project.

## Limits and Constraints

IssueMedic is limited when required information is missing, inaccessible, generated dynamically, or incomplete.
